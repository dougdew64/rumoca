## Authored to trigger

A cross-domain power train — electrical → rotational → translational — with two **ideal**
(rigid) gears.

Authored to trigger the phenomenon every earlier specimen avoids: **high differential index**.
Rigid ratio constraints between components that each carry their own inertia make the
accelerations dependent, which pushes the DAE above index 1.
