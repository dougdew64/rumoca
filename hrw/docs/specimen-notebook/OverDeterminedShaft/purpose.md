# OverDeterminedShaft — why this specimen exists

**Intent, not explanation.** *How* this specimen behaves is regenerated on demand from
[`trace/`](trace/) — generated by `cargo run -p hrw --example gen_trace -- OverDeterminedShaft`, and
therefore correct by construction. What **cannot** be regenerated is why someone made this file —
the code never says what it is *for*.

## Authored to trigger

**The over-determined half of the balance check**: 3 equations, 2 unknowns, reported as
`balance = 1`.

It is the mirror of [`UnbalancedShaft`](../UnbalancedShaft/purpose.md), which is short one equation
and reports `balance = -1`. Both fail at **DAE construction** on the same check (MLS §4.9), and
together they make the sign in that message mean something.

## Authored because half a lesson was untestable

`dae-construction.md` Act 5 reads the sign as informative — negative means a variable with nothing
to determine it, positive means something determined twice. Found 2026-08-17: **every unbalanced
specimen in the corpus reported `-1`**, so the positive half was prose a reader had to accept. Now
it is a stop.

## The surplus equation is CONSISTENT, deliberately

`w = der(phi)` says exactly what `der(phi) = w` already said. There is no contradiction to find,
and **the compiler rejects the model anyway** — because the balance check is arithmetic on counts
and never asks whether the surplus equation agrees.

That isolates one lesson. A contradictory third equation would also be rejected, and would blur
*"one equation too many"* with *"these equations disagree"* — which are different diagnoses needing
different fixes.

**DO NOT FIX.** Repairing it deletes the test.
